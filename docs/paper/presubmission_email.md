# Cell Genomics Presubmission Email Draft

To: cellgenomics@cell.com

Subject: Presubmission inquiry: Triplex-informed lncRNA–gene candidate networks reveal edge-level conservation and rewiring across primates

Dear Cell Genomics editorial team,

We would like to ask whether Cell Genomics would consider a full submission of our manuscript, "Triplex-informed lncRNA–gene candidate networks reveal edge-level conservation and rewiring across primates."

Trait-associated lncRNA and protein-coding gene catalogs nominate molecular nodes, but they do not specify candidate lncRNA–PCG edges or explain how trait-linked programs are conserved or rewired across species. We mapped orthologous lncRNAs and protein-coding genes across human, chimpanzee, macaque, and marmoset, and reconstructed 804,630 predicted lncRNA–PCG relationships with triplex-informed inference. The central comparative result is that node conservation is not edge conservation: human-chimpanzee node Jaccard 0.79 versus edge Jaccard 0.37, and 8,799 observed four-species core-pair edges versus a target-permutation null mean 91.4 and p95 approximately 107 (empirical p < 0.001).

The manuscript makes three linked contributions. First, it converts trait-associated lncRNA and PCG catalogs from node lists into an orthology-aware edge layer across four primates. Second, Figure 3 establishes edge-level lncRNA–PCG conservation and rewiring as a comparative discovery signal distinct from node-level orthology, including target-permutation and degree-bin matched null calibration. Third, Figure 6 integrates trait-gene provenance, GTEx PCG target-program expression context, GO-based module-coherence benchmarking, and matched-null analyses as external benchmarking and contextualization rather than causal evidence.

We believe the study fits Cell Genomics because it is a computational and comparative genomics manuscript centered on a conceptual advance rather than a database-only resource. The Human LncRNA Atlas Companion, a local-only analysis interface, is positioned as a reproducibility and evidence-inspection layer for reviewers and readers, with figure-panel manifests and rebuild commands available in the repository package. We would welcome guidance on whether the current evidence package is appropriate for full submission, or whether additional orthogonal public-data enrichment or wet-lab confirmation would be important before submission.
